# Governance

Ceremony has one maintainer. Tyler Kendrick ([@Tyler-R-Kendrick](https://github.com/Tyler-R-Kendrick)) is the BDFL. There is no foundation, company board, or committee.

## How decisions are made

The maintainer decides scope, design, and what merges. Proposals and discussion belong in issues and pull requests, with the design and evidence this repository already requires. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Discussion informs the decision. Agreement from other contributors is welcome and is not required for the maintainer to decide.

## Committers and maintainers

Commit and maintainer access is by invitation from the maintainer after demonstrated merit on this repository: reviewed work that holds up under the project's checks. Opening issues or pull requests does not grant commit rights. There is no application form and no vote.

## Disputes

Discuss a disagreement in the issue or pull request where it arose. If that discussion does not settle it, the maintainer makes the final call. Conduct reports follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Vulnerability reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Releases

Releases are Git tags. The release workflow and `verify:release` are evidence gates in this repository. They are not labels to set by hand. A passing build or a merge does not authorize publication, provider registration, paid resources, or production deployment. The npm package is not yet published. The package remains private until the maintainer publishes through those gates.

## Continuity

The maintainer may name a successor. That person becomes the maintainer when the maintainer transfers responsibility. If the maintainer is inactive and has not named a successor, continuity is a transfer of the repository to an organization. No one else appoints a new maintainer in the meantime.
